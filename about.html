About me

My story

I got into programming because I liked solving puzzles, and I still do: I spend some of my free time on algorithm problems just for fun.

I started my degree at Mt. San Antonio College and transferred to UC San Diego, where I study Computer Science. These days I’m most interested in building complete products, not just the backend or the frontend, but how the whole thing fits together.

I’m looking for software engineering internships and part-time roles where I can ship real features and learn from experienced engineers.

My path so far

  3. Transferred to UC San Diego.
