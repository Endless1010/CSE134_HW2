Extra credit: MathML

Einstein’s mass–energy equivalence:

E = m c 2

MathML has been part of the HTML standard for years, but developers couldn’t rely on it until recently because browser support was patchy. Firefox has supported it for a long time and Safari supported much of it, but Chrome only shipped it briefly in 2013 before removing it. Chromium-based browsers, including Chrome and Edge, didn’t support MathML Core until version 109 in January 2023. Until then, developers needed libraries like MathJax or images of equations to display math reliably for most users.
